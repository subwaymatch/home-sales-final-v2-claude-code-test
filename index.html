College-town home sales: ten questions

… home sales around 11 university campuses in 18 cities and towns. Each answer below is computed in your browser from data/college_town_home_sales.csv when the page loads. Nothing is typed in by hand.

  1. Q1. Highest median price
  2. Q2. Mean above median
  3. Q3. Rank by mean, two ways
  4. Q4. Campus price change, 2024 to 2025
  5. Q5. Days on market by campus
  6. Q6. Tax burden by campus
  7. Q7. Lowest median price by bedrooms
  8. Q8. Shortest days on market by price range
  9. Q9. Monthly sales by city and year
  10. Q10. Distance to campus by budget

Rules used for every number

  • Valid price means a sale price from $10,000 to $5,000,000, inclusive. Q2 and Q3 also use all rows, and Q9 uses all rows with a sale date.
  • City is city and state together, such as "Champaign, IL" (18 cities). Campus area is the university column (11 areas).
  • Days on market is contract date minus list date, in days, for rows that have both dates. A blank date is not counted as zero.
  • Year and month come from the sale date.
  • Median is used unless a question asks for a mean. Ties are reported in full.
  • Dollars are rounded to whole dollars. Days, miles and percents are rounded to one decimal. Rounding is half up.
  • Rows are never removed for sharing a street address. listing_id is the key.

Colors in every chart: orange marks the cities or campus areas named in the answer. Grey marks everything else.
